Our Love Story

What started as a simple digital hello soon revealed that we had been walking the same school hallways and living in neighboring villages all along. That one click turned into endless conversations, shared dreams, and a love that has grown deeper through over 10+ beautiful years together. From spontaneous road trips to quiet evenings under the stars, we discovered that we were meant to walk through life together.

Now, surrounded by the love of our family and friends, we're ready to begin our greatest adventure yet. Join us as we promise forever to each other and celebrate the beautiful journey that brought us here.
